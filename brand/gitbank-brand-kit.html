GITBANK

Brand Standards · Volume I

The private bank of the commit.

Edition of one · Set in Marcellus, Jost & IBM Plex Mono · MMXXVI

Gitbank is a bank that lives where the work lives. No branches, no lobbies, no wallet pop-ups — a vault on Base, anchored to a GitHub identity, commanded in plain language from the middle of a pull request. The identity holds itself to the same standard: the discretion of a Geneva private bank, drawn with the precision of a ledger.

Two materials carry everything. Obsidian — the near-black of a vault door — and champagne foil, spent sparingly, the way a bank spends trust. The engraved voice is Marcellus; the speaking voice is Jost; and every number, hash and receipt is set in IBM Plex Mono, because the ledger is the product.

Identity

One mark, one voice, no ornament it hasn’t earned.

PLATE I

The Crest

Engraving · 1 color · min. 16 px

A G drawn as a vault ring. The stroke leaves the dial, curves the full circumference, and returns as the crossbar — a branch that merges back to main. Two nodes mark it: the commit at the crown, the HEAD where the bar meets the vault.

PLATE II

The Lockup

Horizontal · reversed · clear space = 1 node

The wordmark is set in Marcellus, tracked wide and never bolded — engraved plates don’t shout. On light stock the mark turns vault green; foil is reserved for obsidian.
